FIELD MANUAL

Code is your weapon.
Memory is the battlefield.

Each bot executes one instruction per turn. Overwrite your opponent’s code with DAT to eliminate it. Cells wrap from 255 back to 0; every address operand is relative to the instruction being executed.

Colors show the bot that loaded or last wrote a cell, not territory or health. A program can survive in cells written by its opponent.

OPCODEOPERATION
DAT a bExecuting this kills the bot. Copying it is harmless.
MOV a bCopy the instruction at PC + a into PC + b.
ADD a bAdd literal a to the b field of the cell at PC + b.
SUB a bSubtract literal a from that destination’s b field.
JMP a bJump to PC + a; ignore b.

Enter one opcode and two integers per line. Use ; for comments. Edits take effect with Reset / apply. Blue always moves first.
